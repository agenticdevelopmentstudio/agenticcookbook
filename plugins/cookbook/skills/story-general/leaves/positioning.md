# Positioning

Positioning frames a project for an audience: audience, purpose, and the value proposition (the elevator pitch and landing copy).

## Audience
- **positioning-name-the-audience**: The narrator MUST identify who the project is for, grounded in the material.

## Purpose
- **positioning-state-the-purpose**: The purpose and goal MUST be stated from evidence — the project's own docs and goals — not guessed.

## Value Proposition
- **positioning-value-proposition**: The value proposition MUST express a concrete benefit (the elevator pitch / landing copy) grounded in real capabilities, avoiding hype the record does not support.
