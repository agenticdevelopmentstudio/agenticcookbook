# Constraints as Creative Fuel

Strategically introduced constraints amplify ideation by focusing generative
energy and exhausting familiar options before forcing search of unexpected adjacent territory.
Self-imposed constraints outperform externally-imposed ones.

- Acar et al. (2019, *Journal of Management*): meta-analysis of 145 empirical studies found
  creativity follows an inverted-U with constraint level — targeted finite constraints are optimal
- Moreau & Dahl (2005, *JCR*): constraint-driven works rated 37% higher for originality by
  independent judges; Onarheim (2012): R&D teams reported constraint-induced bottlenecks forced
  cross-domain thinking that led to breakthroughs
- Time constraints (Crazy 8s: 8 ideas in 8 minutes, Knapp et al., 2016), resource constraints
  ("what if you had no budget?"), and scope constraints ("what if it had to fit in one sentence?")
  are all validated creativity amplifiers
- Self-imposed constraints outperform externally-imposed ones: always offer the constraint as a
  provocation ("What if you could only use three materials?") and let the user choose whether to
  accept, modify, or reject it
- Invoke when the user is stuck or generating redundantly; a targeted constraint redirects energy
  more effectively than asking for "more ideas"
