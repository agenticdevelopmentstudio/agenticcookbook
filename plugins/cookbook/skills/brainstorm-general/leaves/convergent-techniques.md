# Convergent Techniques

Techniques for the Critique and Converge phases — clustering, feasibility assessment, premortem,
and user-led selection. MUST NOT be introduced until divergence is complete.

## Affinity Clustering (KJ Method)

Organizing ideas by natural affinity.

- **convergence-after-divergence**: Facilitator MUST NOT introduce any convergent technique until
  the user explicitly signals readiness to stop generating.
- **affinity-user-words**: Cluster names MUST be written in the user's own language, not
  AI-generated labels; facilitator proposes, user confirms.
- **affinity-small-first**: Clustering SHOULD proceed from ideas to clusters, never from
  predefined clusters to ideas.

## What Would Have to Be True (WWHTBT)

Interrogative framing to assess ideas.

- **wwhtbt-question-form**: The technique MUST be framed as "What would have to be true for
  [idea] to work?" — never as "Why [idea] won't work".
- **wwhtbt-follow-up**: After identifying a condition, facilitator MUST offer to explore how that
  condition might be met before moving to the next idea.

## Premortem

Prospective failure analysis.

- **premortem-before-commit**: Facilitator SHOULD run a premortem before any crystallize step when
  the domain carries high consequences.
- **premortem-generate-then-address**: Premortem MUST complete failure-mode generation before
  addressing any failure mode; mixing them reintroduces premature convergence.
- **premortem-round-robin**: In the premortem, facilitator SHOULD surface all failure mechanisms
  before ranking or addressing any.

## Feasibility Check

Structured pragmatist critique.

- **feasibility-three-axes**: Feasibility check MUST cover all three axes: assumptions (what is
  being taken for granted), risks (what could go wrong), and dependencies (what would be needed to
  succeed).
- **feasibility-question-form**: Every feasibility observation MUST be framed as a question or a
  condition, not a verdict.

## Idea Selection (user-led)

- **selection-user-led**: Convergent selection of a direction MUST be user-led; facilitator may
  surface evaluation criteria but MUST NOT make or imply the final selection.
- **selection-criteria-explicit**: Before selection, facilitator SHOULD invite the user to name
  explicit selection criteria ("What matters most to you when choosing?").
