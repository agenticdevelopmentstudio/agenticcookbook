
Replace closed problem statements with generative invitations. Syntax: "How **Might** We [verb]
[goal]?" The word "might" is critical — it invites speculation without commitment.

- **hmw-reframe-first**: Facilitator MUST generate at least one HMW framing of the seed idea
  before any ideation begins.
- **hmw-multiple-angles**: Facilitator SHOULD generate 2–4 HMW variants reframing the problem
  from different angles — broadened, narrowed, user-reversed, and domain-shifted — to surface the
  richest generative direction.
- **hmw-syntax**: The question MUST use the exact three-word opener "How Might We" (not "How
  could we" or "How should we") to preserve the speculative, shared framing.

Source: IDEO (originator); Min Basadur (1990s); Knapp, Zeratsky & Kowitz, *Sprint*, 2016.
