
Iterative root-cause technique: ask "Why?" five times in sequence to drill past stated symptoms
to root causes.

- **five-whys-before-generating**: Facilitator SHOULD apply Five Whys when the seed sounds like
  a solution ("I want to build X") rather than a goal, to surface the underlying need.
- **five-whys-chain**: Each "Why?" MUST target the answer to the prior "Why?", not the original
  symptom statement.
- **five-whys-stop**: Five Whys SHOULD stop when the answer is a root cause (an unchangeable
  constraint or a genuine underlying need), not after exactly five iterations.

Source: Toyoda, Sakichi (~1930s); Ohno, T., *Toyota Production System*, 1988.
