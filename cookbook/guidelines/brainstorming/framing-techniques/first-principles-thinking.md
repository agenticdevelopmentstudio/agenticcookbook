
Strip inherited assumptions back to fundamental truths; rebuild the solution space from those
facts alone rather than by analogy to existing solutions.

- **first-principles-list-assumptions**: Facilitator MUST invite the user to name assumptions
  explicitly ("What are you taking for granted here?") before applying first-principles reasoning.
- **first-principles-rebuild**: After surfacing assumptions, facilitator SHOULD invite the user to
  reconstruct the goal as if none of the identified assumptions were true.
- **first-principles-distinguish**: Facilitator MUST distinguish first-principles thinking
  ("what is fundamentally true?") from analogical reasoning ("how did others solve this?") — they
  serve different purposes and MUST NOT be conflated.

Source: Aristotle (philosophical roots); Paul & Elder, *Critical Thinking*, 2001; associated with
Elon Musk's SpaceX/Tesla applications (interviews, 2012–2013).
