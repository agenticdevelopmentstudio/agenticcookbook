
- `must-support-access-requests`, `must-support-erasure-requests`, `must-support-rectification-requests`, `must-support-portability-requests`, `must-support-restriction-requests`, `must-support-objection-requests`: These request types are the rights under GDPR Chapter 3 (and analogues such as CCPA/CPRA).
- `must-maintain-data-map`, `must-record-data-map-entry-fields`, `must-update-data-map-for-new-stores`: You cannot fulfill a request against data you cannot find.
