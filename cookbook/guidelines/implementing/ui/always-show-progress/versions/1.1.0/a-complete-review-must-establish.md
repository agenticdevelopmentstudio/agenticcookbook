
- `must-show-determinate-progress-when-total-known`: whether every async wait with known total work shows determinate progress (progress bar with percentage)
- `must-show-indeterminate-progress-when-total-unknown`: whether every async wait with unknown total work shows indeterminate progress (spinner, skeleton, shimmer)
- `must-not-appear-frozen`: whether the UI can appear frozen or unresponsive during any async wait
