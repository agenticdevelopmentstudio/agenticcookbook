
- `must-show-determinate-progress-when-total-known`: every async wait whose total work is known shows a progress bar with percentage
- `must-show-indeterminate-progress-when-total-unknown`: every async wait whose total work is not known shows a spinner, skeleton or shimmer
- `must-not-appear-frozen`: no async wait leaves the UI frozen or unresponsive
- `should-update-progress-bar-as-work-completes`: every progress bar advances as work completes
