
- `should-use-semantic-color-tokens`: every color is a platform semantic color, or the work says why a hex value is used
- `should-limit-the-palette`: the palette holds 1 primary/accent color, 1-2 neutral tones and semantic success/warning/error colors, or the work says why not
- `must-not-rely-on-color-alone`: every state change, error and status has an icon, shape, text or pattern beside its color
- `must-meet-contrast-minimums`: normal text is at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1 and non-text UI components at least 3:1
- `must-work-in-light-and-dark`: every color works in both light and dark themes
- `should-test-light-and-dark`: the UI was tested in both light and dark themes, or the work says why not
