
- `must-gate-adoption-on-baseline`, `may-use-widely-available-without-fallback`, `should-fallback-for-newly-available`, `should-date-baseline-claims`, `should-verify-baseline-status`: these are the requirements for deciding adoption with Baseline.
- `should-use-cross-document-transitions-only-as-enhancement`, `must-not-make-navigation-depend-on-cross-document-transitions`: **Cross-document (MPA) View Transitions are a FORECAST for full interoperability** — they ship in Chromium and Safari but not Firefox as of this writing, so they are NOT Baseline.
