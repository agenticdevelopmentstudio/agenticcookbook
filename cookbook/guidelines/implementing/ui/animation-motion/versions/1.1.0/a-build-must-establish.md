
- `should-make-motion-purposeful`: every animation guides attention, shows a spatial relationship or provides feedback, or the work says why not
- `must-not-animate-for-decoration`: no animation is purely decorative
- `should-use-duration-defaults`: every duration without a platform value falls in the default range for its interaction, or the work says why not
- `should-prefer-platform-curves`: every animation uses a platform-native spring/easing curve, or the work says why not
- `must-respect-reduced-motion`: with reduced motion enabled, every animation becomes an instant state change or simple cross-fade
- `should-not-cover-large-distances-loop-or-flash`: no motion covers a large distance, loops continuously or flashes, or the work says why
