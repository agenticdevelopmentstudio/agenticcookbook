
- `must-size-targets-for-accurate-input`: an interactive element too small to tap or click accurately
- `should-defer-to-platform-hig-minimum`: a target below its platform HIG minimum (44x44 pt iOS, 48x48 dp Android, 32x32 epx Windows, 24x24 CSS px web)
- `should-default-to-44x44`: a target below 44x44 where no platform HIG applies
- `should-pad-hit-area-beyond-visual`: a small icon or text whose hit area is not padded out to the target size
- `must-space-adjacent-targets-8px`: adjacent targets closer than 8px
- `should-give-inline-links-line-height`: inline text links in paragraphs with line height too tight for comfortable tapping
