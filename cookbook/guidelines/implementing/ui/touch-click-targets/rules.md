
- **must-size-targets-for-accurate-input**: Interactive elements MUST be large enough to tap or click accurately.
- **should-defer-to-platform-hig-minimum**: SHOULD defer to the platform HIG first — each prescribes its own minimum (see the platform minimums table).
- **should-default-to-44x44**: **Cross-platform default: 44x44** when no platform HIG applies — targets SHOULD be at least 44x44 then.
- **should-pad-hit-area-beyond-visual**: The visual element (icon, text) MAY be smaller than the touch target — the hit area SHOULD then be padded to the target size.
- **must-space-adjacent-targets-8px**: There MUST be minimum **8px spacing** between adjacent targets to prevent mis-taps.
- **should-give-inline-links-line-height**: Inline text links in paragraphs are exempt from size minimums but SHOULD have sufficient line height for comfortable tapping.
