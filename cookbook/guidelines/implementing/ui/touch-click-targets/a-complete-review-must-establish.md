
- `must-size-targets-for-accurate-input`: whether every interactive element is large enough to tap or click accurately
- `should-defer-to-platform-hig-minimum`: whether each target meets its platform HIG minimum
- `should-default-to-44x44`: whether targets are at least 44x44 where no platform HIG applies
- `should-pad-hit-area-beyond-visual`: whether visual elements smaller than the target have a padded hit area
- `must-space-adjacent-targets-8px`: whether adjacent targets have at least 8px spacing
- `should-give-inline-links-line-height`: whether inline text links have sufficient line height for comfortable tapping
- `should-defer-to-platform-hig-minimum`: Interactive targets meet at least the Web/WCAG minimum of 24×24 CSS px (44×44 recommended; the relevant platform HIG minimum where one applies, and the 44×44 default when none does);
