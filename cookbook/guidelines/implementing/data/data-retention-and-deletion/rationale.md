
- `must-define-retention-period`, `should-maintain-retention-schedule`, `should-automate-expiry`: Storage limitation (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e), as of the 2016 regulation) requires that data be kept no longer than necessary for its purpose.
- `should-cascade-deletions`, `should-maintain-copy-inventory`, `must-make-failed-cascade-resumable`: A deletion that misses a copy is not a deletion.
