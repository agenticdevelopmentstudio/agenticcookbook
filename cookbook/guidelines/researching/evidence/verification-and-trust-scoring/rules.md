
- **should-verify-with-the-domain-framework**: A claim SHOULD be verified with the framework for its domain.
- **should-score-on-the-common-scale**: A claim SHOULD be scored on the common 0-100 scale (see *Scoring (0-100)*).
- **must-apply-the-domain-ceiling**: The score MUST then apply a **domain ceiling** that caps a claim failing its domain's minimum source — e.g. a medical claim caps near 45 without a peer-reviewed RCT or systematic review, however confident it sounds.
- **should-score-false-positive-patterns-low**: A claim SHOULD score LOW when it is: single-source; citation laundering / loops; confident-but-unsupported; published in a predatory venue; carrying an undisclosed conflict of interest; wrapped in urgency / emotional framing; outdated in a fast-moving domain; an anecdote generalized to a population; viral-but-unverified; backed by a fabricated or hallucinated citation; from an authority speaking outside their field; p-hacked / unreplicated; or AI content-farm output.
