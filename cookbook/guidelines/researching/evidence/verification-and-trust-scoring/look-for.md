
- `should-verify-with-the-domain-framework`: a claim verified with no framework, or one that does not fit its domain
- `should-score-on-the-common-scale`: a score that does not blend authority, corroboration, accuracy, currency and transparency on the 0-100 scale
- `must-apply-the-domain-ceiling`: a claim scored above its domain ceiling while failing that domain's minimum source
- `should-score-false-positive-patterns-low`: a claim showing a catalogued false-positive pattern (single-source, laundered citation, predatory venue, fabricated citation, and the rest) that does not score LOW
