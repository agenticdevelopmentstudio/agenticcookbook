
- `should-capture-provenance-not-prose`: findings written as essay prose with no provenance attached
- `must-carry-the-per-claim-record`: a claim missing any field of the per-claim record: one verifiable sentence, a full citation with access date, source type, exact verbatim quote, generator and query, trust score with basis, or independence of support
- `should-record-the-document-container`: a document missing topic and scope, research questions, methodology, agents and sources, dates, executive summary, findings, open questions, limitations or license
- `should-align-with-provenance-standards`: provenance fields that follow none of the listed standards
