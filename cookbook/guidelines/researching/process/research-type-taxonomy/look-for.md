
- `should-classify-before-search`: a classification step runs before the first search
- `should-apply-three-lenses`: the classification records intent, domain and method
- `should-let-type-pick-sources-method-and-bar`: the sources, method and bar used are the ones the type's row names, not chosen ad hoc
- `should-classify-by-keywords-and-intent`: classification reads the topic's keywords and its intent
- `must-escalate-bar-for-high-stakes`: when life or safety, legal or financial stakes appear, or the answer will be acted on, the bar is one level above the type's own; a bar left at the type's level there breaks it
- `must-let-higher-stakes-type-set-bar`: a topic spanning types is held to the bar of its highest-stakes type
