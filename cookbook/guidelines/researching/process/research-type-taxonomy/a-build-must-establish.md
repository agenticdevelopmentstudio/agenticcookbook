
- `should-classify-before-search`: the topic's classification exists before the first search, or the work says why not
- `should-apply-three-lenses`: the classification names the topic's intent, domain and method, or the work says why not
- `should-let-type-pick-sources-method-and-bar`: the sources, method and bar match the type's row, or the work says why not
- `should-classify-by-keywords-and-intent`: the classification cites the keywords and intent it rests on, or the work says why not
- `must-escalate-bar-for-high-stakes`: every topic with life or safety, legal or financial stakes, or whose answer will be acted on, carries a bar one level above its type's
- `must-let-higher-stakes-type-set-bar`: every topic spanning types carries the bar of its higher-stakes type
