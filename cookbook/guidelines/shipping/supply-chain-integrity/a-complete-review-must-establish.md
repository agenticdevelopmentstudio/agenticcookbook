
- `must-produce-sbom`: whether every release build produces an SBOM listing every component and version
- `should-use-one-sbom-format`: whether one SBOM format, CycloneDX or SPDX, is used consistently
- `must-produce-signed-provenance`: whether every release build produces signed build provenance
- `should-generate-sbom-from-lockfile`: whether the SBOM comes from the resolved dependency graph
- `should-generate-provenance-on-hosted-ci`: whether provenance is generated by the hosted CI platform itself
- `should-sign-artifacts-and-attestations`: whether artifacts and attestations are signed
- `should-pin-toolchain-by-digest`: whether the toolchain and base images are pinned by digest
- `should-target-slsa-l2`: whether the build targets SLSA Build L2, or L3 where the threat model justifies it
- `must-verify-signature-before-deploy`: whether the deploy step verifies the signature against the expected signer identity
- `should-verify-provenance-before-deploy`: whether the deploy step verifies provenance
- `must-block-promotion-on-failed-check`: whether a failed check blocks promotion rather than warning
- `should-store-release-evidence`: whether SBOMs and attestations are stored as immutable release evidence
- `should-commit-lockfiles`: whether lockfiles are committed and CI installs frozen/locked
- `should-pin-dependencies-by-digest`: whether dependencies and CI actions are pinned, ideally by digest
- `should-keep-scanning-for-vulnerable-components`: whether scanning for known-vulnerable components continues
- `must-not-ship-without-sbom-and-provenance`: whether any release ships with no SBOM and no provenance
- `must-not-pass-unverifiable-attestation`: whether an unsigned or unverifiable attestation is treated as passing
- `must-not-claim-hosted-trust-for-author-controlled-provenance`: whether L2 trust is claimed for author-controlled provenance
- `should-confirm-regulatory-applicability`: whether regulatory applicability was confirmed for the jurisdiction and sector
