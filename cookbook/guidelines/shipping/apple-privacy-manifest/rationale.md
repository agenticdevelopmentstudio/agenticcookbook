
- `should-keep-manifest-minimal-and-accurate`, `must-not-declare-unused-categories`, `must-declare-every-reached-category`: The manifest is additive and durable: declaring an unused category or a missing reason both cause rejection.
- `should-recheck-docs-for-new-foundation-calls`, `should-treat-unpublished-category-changes-as-forecast`: Apple may extend the required-reason API list over time.
