
- **must-not-assume-a-single-regime**: You **MUST NOT** assume a single regime covers all users — most products are multi-jurisdictional.
- **should-pin-and-recheck-the-applicability-matrix**: You SHOULD treat the US state landscape as a moving target: pin your applicability matrix to a dated revision and **SHOULD** re-check quarterly.

These appear across GDPR and most US state laws, so build to them once:

- **must-record-a-lawful-basis**: **lawful basis**: You **MUST** establish and record a lawful basis (GDPR) or required notice/opt-out posture (US) before collection.
- **must-collect-only-needed-data**: **data minimization**: You **MUST** collect only data needed for the stated purpose.
- **should-default-to-not-collecting**: You SHOULD default to *not* collecting.
- **must-limit-use-to-disclosed-purposes**: **purpose limitation**: You **MUST** use data only for the purposes disclosed at collection. New purpose requires new basis/notice.
- **must-define-retention-per-data-type**: **storage limitation**: You **MUST** define retention per data type and delete or anonymize when it expires.
- **must-disclose-at-the-point-of-collection**: **transparency**: You **MUST** disclose what is collected, why, and with whom it is shared, in plain language at the point of collection.
- **should-design-for-data-subject-rights**: **data subject rights**: You **SHOULD** design for access, correction, deletion, and opt-out from the start (see [adc.guideline.planning.security.privacy-by-design](cookbook:09b063bd-74e7-4636-baa6-23ee82ec224b)).

- **must-identify-controller-or-processor-role**: You **MUST** identify which role each system plays before design.
- **must-govern-processors-with-a-dpa**: Processor relationships **MUST** be governed by a written data processing agreement (DPA).
- **should-determine-applicable-regimes-before-collecting**: Before you collect, you SHOULD determine applicable regimes from **user geography** and **data types** (special-category/sensitive data raises the bar).
- **should-confirm-the-lawful-basis-before-collecting**: Before you collect, you SHOULD confirm a lawful basis or required notice/opt-out posture — and record it.
- **should-confirm-minimization-retention-and-deletion-per-field**: Before you collect, you SHOULD confirm minimization, retention, and a deletion path exist for each field.
- **should-consult-counsel**: You **SHOULD** consult counsel for binding interpretation.
- **must-not-treat-this-as-legal-sign-off**: Agents **MUST NOT** treat this guideline as legal sign-off.
