
- `should-treat-subscription-owners-as-lifecycle-coupled`: A's lifecycle bounds the subscription.
- `may-separate-weakly-referenced-objects`: A `weak` reference from A to B means B does not own A.
- `should-group-owner-with-owned-objects`, `should-group-session-with-its-objects`: Objects that are born together, live together, and die together form a natural unit.
