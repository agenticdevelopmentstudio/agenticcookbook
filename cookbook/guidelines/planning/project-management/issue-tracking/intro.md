
# Issue Tracking

Keep every project issue in one tracker and triage each new one promptly: a named triager gives it one owner and assesses its severity by impact, apart from its priority. Track its status to a closed state, escalate critical issues and issues left unresolved too long by a documented path, and close each issue with a recorded resolution reason, a link to the fixing change and a confirmed fix.

This guideline covers project issues and defects from report to close, for any delivery team and any tracker. A team conforms to it when its tracker, its triage and its closes meet the rules below. Production incidents are covered by [adc.guideline.shipping.incident-response](cookbook:2041fbd9-d210-485e-9a3a-b2d8295ed924), security vulnerability triage by [adc.guideline.reviewing.security.vulnerability-prioritization](cookbook:86671e07-24b3-476c-88be-91ed8f83eb30), risks that have not happened by [adc.guideline.planning.project-management.risk-tracking](cookbook:a55182ef-3144-407c-b64a-6377f37f5c63), and the general convention for linking project records by [adc.guideline.planning.project-management.project-record-linking](cookbook:23e55c7c-c16e-40d1-88a0-4b51148d2660).

The rule `link-issues-to-findings`, kept as the owner's text, uses its own term: a *specialist finding* is a finding reported by a specialist reviewer, one of a team of reviewers that each check one subject, such as security or accessibility.
