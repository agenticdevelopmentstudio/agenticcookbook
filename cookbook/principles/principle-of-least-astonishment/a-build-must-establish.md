
- `should-match-caller-expectations`: the APIs, UI, and system behavior match what users and callers expect, or the work says why not
- `must-deliver-the-behavior-a-name-suggests`: every name delivers the behavior it suggests
- `should-make-side-effects-obvious-from-signature`: every side effect is obvious from the API signature, or the work says why not
- `should-name-for-predictability`: every function and type is named so a reader can predict what it does without reading the implementation, or the work says why not
- `must-make-mutation-visible`: every method that mutates state shows it in its name or signature, and no side effect is hidden
- `should-follow-platform-naming-conventions`: names follow the platform's naming conventions, or the work says why not
- `should-ask-would-a-new-team-member-be-surprised`: no behavior in the work would surprise a new team member, or the work says why it must
