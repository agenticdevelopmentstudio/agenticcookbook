
- `should-spend-real-time-on-problem-definition`: The Double Diamond (Design Council, 2005) devotes its entire first diamond to finding the right problem before the second diamond seeks solutions.
- `should-use-how-might-we-framing`: "How *Might* We [verb] [goal]?" (IDEO; Min Basadur, 1990s) assumes solvability, invites speculation without commitment, and creates shared ownership.
- `should-apply-five-whys`: Five Whys comes from Toyoda/Ohno and the Toyota Production System (1930s).
- `should-use-first-principles-thinking`: First-principles thinking follows the Stanford d.school Empathize→Define (2010), Aristotle, and Musk/SpaceX.
- `should-spend-real-time-on-problem-definition`, `should-apply-five-whys`, `should-use-first-principles-thinking`: A good reframe often reveals that the originally-stated goal was solving the wrong level of problem.
