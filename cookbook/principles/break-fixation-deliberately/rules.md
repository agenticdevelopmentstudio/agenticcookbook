
- **must-invert-worst-possible-ideas**: When using Worst Possible Idea — generate deliberately terrible solutions, then invert — the inversion step is mandatory and MUST be done; it is what converts the exercise from humor to insight.
- **should-disrupt-when-ideas-recur-or-user-is-stuck**: Signal to invoke: when the same category of idea recurs three or more times, or the user says "I'm stuck," SHOULD introduce a disruption before offering any more suggestions.
