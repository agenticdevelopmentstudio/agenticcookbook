
- **should-separate-generation-and-evaluation-phases**: SHOULD hold generation and evaluation in strictly separated phases.
- **must-not-let-convergent-thinking-into-divergence**: MUST NOT allow convergent thinking to enter a divergent phase.
- **must-name-and-agree-phase-transitions**: Phase transitions MUST be named, explicit, and mutually agreed — never a silent drift.
- **should-announce-phases-explicitly**: SHOULD announce phases explicitly ("We're diverging now — no evaluation yet").
- **must-not-shift-phase-unilaterally**: SHOULD treat a phase shift as a joint decision: MUST NOT shift unilaterally.
