
- `should-separate-generation-and-evaluation-phases`: generation and evaluation mixed in the same phase
- `must-not-let-convergent-thinking-into-divergence`: evaluation, ranking, or selection inside a divergent phase
- `must-name-and-agree-phase-transitions`: a phase transition that happened by silent drift
- `should-announce-phases-explicitly`: a phase begun without being announced
- `must-not-shift-phase-unilaterally`: a phase shift made by one party alone
