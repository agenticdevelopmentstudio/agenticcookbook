
- `should-compose-from-small-focused-pieces`: new behavior is composed from small, focused pieces, or the work says why not
- `should-use-inheritance-only-for-genuine-is-a`: every subclass added stands for a genuine "is-a" relationship, or the work says why not
- `should-prefer-protocols-over-base-classes`: shared behavior is extracted into protocols/interfaces or standalone functions, not a base class, or the work says why not
- `should-wrap-rather-than-subclass`: added behavior wraps an existing type in a new type rather than subclassing it, or the work says why not
- `must-not-nest-inheritance-more-than-one-level`: no inheritance hierarchy is more than 1 level deep, except where each subclass can stand in everywhere its parent is used
