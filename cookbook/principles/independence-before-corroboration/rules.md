
- **should-count-sources-by-independence**: Sources SHOULD be counted by their independence, not their number — many pages tracing back to one origin are still one source.
- **should-check-sources-share-no-origin**: Before a claim is treated as corroborated, its sources SHOULD be checked to confirm they do not share an origin.
- **should-record-corroboration-level**: Each claim's corroboration SHOULD be recorded as independent, single, or uncorroborated.
