
- `must-invite-human-ideas-first`: AI-generated ideas or alternatives offered before the human was invited to voice their own
- `must-ask-before-offering-ideas`: a session that opens with "Here's an idea" rather than "What have you been thinking?"
- `may-add-after-human-contributes`: AI additions that replace the human's contribution instead of building on it with "yes-and"
- `must-not-anchor-or-supplant`: an AI addition that sets the anchor for the thinking or supplants what the human contributed
