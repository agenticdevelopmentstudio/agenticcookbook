
- `should-score-claims-by-evidence`: every claim's score is derived from its evidence, not from how certain its wording sounds, or the work says why not
- `should-lower-trust-for-uncited-certainty`: every claim using certainty words ("proven", "scientists confirm") without a supporting citation has its trust lowered, not raised, or the work says why not
- `may-surface-unsupported-claims-scored-low`: every popular-but-unsupported claim that is surfaced carries a low score
- `should-not-present-unsupported-claims-as-true`: no popular-but-unsupported claim is presented as true, or the work says why
- `should-match-confidence-to-sources`: every stated confidence matches the strength and independence of its sources, or the work says why not
- `should-scrutinize-urgency-and-emotion`: every urgent or emotionally framed source was scrutinized harder, not believed on its framing, or the work says why not
