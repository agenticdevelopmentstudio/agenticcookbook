
- **should-score-claims-by-evidence**: A claim SHOULD be scored by its evidence, not by how certain it sounds.
- **should-lower-trust-for-uncited-certainty**: Words like "proven" or "scientists confirm" without a supporting citation SHOULD lower trust, not raise it.
- **may-surface-unsupported-claims-scored-low**: A popular-but-unsupported claim MAY be surfaced and scored low; doing so is useful.
- **should-not-present-unsupported-claims-as-true**: A popular-but-unsupported claim SHOULD NOT be presented as true.
- **should-match-confidence-to-sources**: Stated confidence SHOULD match the strength and independence of the sources.
- **should-scrutinize-urgency-and-emotion**: Urgency and emotional framing SHOULD be treated as signals to scrutinize harder, not to believe.
