
- `should-support-human-autonomy`: Shneiderman (2020, *Human-Centered AI*) sets human autonomy, competence, and oversight as the aim, with the human as author.

- `should-support-human-autonomy`, `should-scaffold-at-zpd`: HAICo (Dong et al., 2024, arXiv 2512.18388): the scaffolding posture (facilitate exploration before generating) produced 42% more novel outputs than the generation posture.
- `should-use-yes-and-stance`, `must-not-replace-human-framing`: The "yes-and" stance comes from Sawyer (2007) and Hatcher (2018).
