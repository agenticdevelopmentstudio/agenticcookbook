
- `should-critique-after-divergence-is-complete`: critique offered while ideas are still being generated
- `should-critique-with-questions-not-verdicts`: a critique delivered as a verdict ("This won't work") rather than a question
- `should-target-the-idea-not-the-person`: a challenge aimed at the person who proposed an idea
- `must-propose-alternative-after-every-objection`: an objection raised with no alternative direction proposed after it
