
- **should-critique-after-divergence-is-complete**: Critique SHOULD be applied after divergence is complete, so it strengthens the idea pool rather than shrinking it.
- **should-critique-with-questions-not-verdicts**: Critique SHOULD take the form of questions ("What would have to be true for this to succeed?"), which preserve the idea for refinement, not verdicts ("This won't work"), which end it.
- **should-target-the-idea-not-the-person**: A challenge SHOULD target the idea, not the person.
- **must-propose-alternative-after-every-objection**: Constructive devil's advocacy MUST propose an alternative direction after every objection.
