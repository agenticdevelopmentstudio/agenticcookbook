
- **should-set-the-bar-by-domain**: The bar a claim must clear SHOULD be set by its domain — the cost of being wrong — not by how casually or urgently it was asked.
- **must-demand-primary-sources-in-high-consequence-domains**: High-consequence domains (medical, legal, financial, scientific) MUST demand independent primary or credentialed sources.
- **may-validate-low-consequence-by-consensus**: Low-consequence, subjective domains (recipes, product picks, opinion) MAY validate by broad independent consensus.
- **must-not-lower-the-bar-for-confident-tone**: A confident tone or expert phrasing MUST NOT lower the bar; the domain does.
- **should-cap-trust-below-domain-minimum**: SHOULD cap a claim's trust score when it fails its domain's minimum source, however plausible it sounds.
