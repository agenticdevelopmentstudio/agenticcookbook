
- `should-set-the-bar-by-domain`: a claim's bar raised or lowered by how casually or urgently it was asked
- `must-demand-primary-sources-in-high-consequence-domains`: a medical, legal, financial, or scientific claim accepted without independent primary or credentialed sources
- `may-validate-low-consequence-by-consensus`: a low-consequence, subjective claim held to a high-consequence bar, or a consensus that is not broad and independent
- `must-not-lower-the-bar-for-confident-tone`: a claim scored up for its confident tone or expert phrasing
- `should-cap-trust-below-domain-minimum`: a plausible claim scored above its cap though it fails its domain's minimum source
