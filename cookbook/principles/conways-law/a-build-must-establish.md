
- `should-shape-boundaries-to-target-architecture`: team and agent boundaries match the intended architecture, or the work says why not
- `should-treat-agent-assignment-as-architecture`: the agent assignment is stated with the module boundaries it is meant to produce, or the work says why not
- `should-optimize-real-coordination-paths`: the real coordination paths are the ones shaped, or the work says why not
- `should-treat-inverse-maneuver-as-lens`: aligned boundaries are not taken as proof of the target architecture, or the work says why not
- `should-verify-emergent-structure`: the emergent structure is compared with the intended design, or the work says why not
- `should-assign-agents-along-desired-seams`: each module or bounded context is assigned to one agent or one tightly-coordinating cluster, or the work says why not
- `should-watch-for-high-bandwidth-handoffs`: each high-bandwidth handoff between agents is named with the coupling expected at its boundary, or the work says why not
- `should-restructure-assignment-not-prompt`: an undesirable handoff coupling is met by restructuring the assignment, not only the prompt, or the work says why not
- `should-repartition-when-architecture-changes`: a desired architectural shift comes with a re-partition of who builds what, or the work says why not
