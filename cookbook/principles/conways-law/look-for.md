
- `should-shape-boundaries-to-target-architecture`: team or agent boundaries set without regard to the architecture wanted
- `should-treat-agent-assignment-as-architecture`: agents assigned to work for scheduling reasons alone, with no thought to the module boundaries it will imprint
- `should-optimize-real-coordination-paths`: boundaries reasoned from nominal reporting lines or agent labels rather than from who actually coordinates with whom
- `should-treat-inverse-maneuver-as-lens`: a target architecture assumed to follow from aligned boundaries alone
- `should-verify-emergent-structure`: no check of the emergent structure against the intended design
- `should-assign-agents-along-desired-seams`: one coherent module split across loosely-coupled agents, or unrelated concerns forced onto one agent
- `should-watch-for-high-bandwidth-handoffs`: two agents exchanging large, frequently-changing context, with the coupling it produces at their boundary left unexamined
- `should-restructure-assignment-not-prompt`: an undesirable coupling from a handoff addressed by changing only the prompt
- `should-repartition-when-architecture-changes`: a desired architectural shift attempted with the old assignment of who builds what left in place
