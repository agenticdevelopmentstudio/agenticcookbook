
- `should-optimize-real-coordination-paths`: **Communication patterns drive the effect — not org charts.** What matters is who actually coordinates with whom (shared context, handoffs, review loops), not nominal reporting lines or agent labels.
- `should-assign-agents-along-desired-seams`: Splitting a single coherent module across loosely-coupled agents tends to fracture it; forcing unrelated concerns onto one agent tends to fuse them.
