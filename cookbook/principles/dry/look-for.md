
- `should-give-each-piece-of-knowledge-one-representation`: the same business rule, constant, schema, or validation written out in more than one place
- `should-ask-whether-same-knowledge-before-deduplicating`: look-alike fragments that encode different rules merged into one, coupling them
- `should-consolidate-facts-across-layers`: the same fact restated by hand in the model, validation, UI copy, or tests
- `should-prefer-derivation-over-synchronized-copies`: hand-synchronized copies of one fact where generation, configuration, or derivation would do
- `should-duplicate-when-unsure`: an abstraction joining fragments whose shared knowledge is in doubt
