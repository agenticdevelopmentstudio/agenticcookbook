
- `must-capture-provenance-while-gathering`: whether each claim's source, exact supporting quote, and access date were captured as it was gathered, not reconstructed afterward
- `should-attach-citation-when-claim-is-made`: whether each claim carried its citation and verbatim supporting quote at the moment it was made
- `should-record-search-and-origin`: whether what was searched and where each claim came from are recorded alongside the conclusion
- `should-not-output-claims-without-provenance`: whether the output holds any claim whose provenance could not be captured
