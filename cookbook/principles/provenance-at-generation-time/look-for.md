
- `must-capture-provenance-while-gathering`: a claim whose source, supporting quote, or access date was filled in after the fact
- `should-attach-citation-when-claim-is-made`: a claim made without its citation and verbatim supporting quote attached
- `should-record-search-and-origin`: a conclusion recorded with no trace of what was searched or where the claim came from
- `should-not-output-claims-without-provenance`: a claim in the output whose provenance could not be captured
