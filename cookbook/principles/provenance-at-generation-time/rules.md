
- **must-capture-provenance-while-gathering**: MUST capture a claim's provenance as you gather it — the source, the exact supporting quote, and the access date — and MUST NOT reconstruct it afterward.
- **should-attach-citation-when-claim-is-made**: SHOULD attach the citation and the verbatim supporting quote at the moment the claim is made.
- **should-record-search-and-origin**: SHOULD record what was searched and where the claim came from, not just the conclusion.
- **should-not-output-claims-without-provenance**: A claim whose provenance cannot be captured SHOULD NOT be in the output; it does not belong there.
