
- `must-grow-pool-before-selection`: Osborn (1953) and Linus Pauling: "the best way to have a good idea is to have lots of ideas";
  IDEO targets ~100 ideas in 60 minutes of ideation
- `should-withhold-evaluation-during-divergence`: Evaluation apprehension — the fear of social judgment — is one of the three primary suppressors
  of group ideation (Diehl & Stroebe, 1987, 1991); withholding evaluation removes this suppressor
- `should-withhold-evaluation-during-divergence`: Amabile's Intrinsic Motivation Principle (1983, 1996): external evaluation during generation
  shifts motivation from intrinsic to extrinsic and measurably suppresses creative output
- `must-grow-pool-before-selection`: Divergent thinking metrics — fluency (quantity), flexibility (category variety), originality
  (novelty), elaboration (detail) — are all maximized *before* convergent selection begins
