# Review: exploratory-testing-charters

## Findings

- `examples.md`, `running-a-chartered-session.md` step 8, `intro.md` paragraph 1: the text drops or makes conditional the expectation that the MUST rule `anomaly-carries-all-four-parts` requires on every anomaly ("where (URL or path), what was seen, what was expected instead, and how repeatable it appeared. An anomaly missing the expectation is a fail"). There are three instances. (1) The example finding has no `Expected:` line, only `Oracle:`, and its `Where: checkout, order confirmation page` is neither a URL nor a path, so the guideline's own illustration fails one of its MUST rules. (2) How-to step 8 says "what was expected (where that is not obvious) or why the behaviour is a problem". That makes the expectation optional, so following the how-to breaks the MUST, and it settles the owner's open question on whether the expectation stays a mandatory fourth part. (3) The intro's summary of a finding lists "where it was seen, the steps to reproduce it, the environment, why it is a problem, and how repeatable it was" and leaves out what was expected. Fix: give the example finding a path (for example `Where: /checkout/confirmation`) and an `Expected:` line (for example "the confirmation page shows the current cart total, 2 items, 30.00"), and keep the `Oracle:` line if wanted. In step 8, list what was expected unconditionally and give why the behaviour is a problem as an addition, not an alternative. Add "what was expected" to the intro's list.
- `examples.md`, the session report with no problems found: the line `Time: 50 minutes on charter, 10 on opportunity (an unexpected currency switch in settings)` records an unexpected behaviour inside a report whose result is "no problems found". The charter puts "account settings" out of scope, and the report neither reports the behaviour as out-of-charter (MUST `findings-stay-inside-charter`) nor leaves it out of the clean result (MUST `clean-pass-reports-coverage`: "Padding a clean pass with severity-free observations is a fail"). The illustration takes a side on two owner open questions: whether an out-of-charter anomaly is raised or only listed as out-of-charter, and whether observations beside a clean result are a fail. Fix: make the opportunity time an off-charter area that held no anomaly and is not excluded by the charter (for example "10 on opportunity (the order-history page linked from the confirmation)"), and change the `New charter noted:` line to match.
- `what-a-reviewer-looks-for.md`: the review focus weakens or leaves out MUST rules. There are three instances. (1) "kept within any step cap" treats the MUST `session-has-hard-step-cap` (25 browser steps per pass) as optional, which leans on the owner's open question on whether a step cap is kept at all. (2) "on the system's own origin" restates `stay-on-charter-origin` ("the site's own origin") in the open question's alternative wording. (3) Nothing checks the `clean-pass-reports-coverage` fail conditions (padding a clean pass with severity-free observations, a silent empty result) or the "URL or path" form of where in `anomaly-carries-all-four-parts`. Fix: write "kept within 25 browser steps per pass and on the site's own origin", and add checks that a clean pass is neither padded with severity-free observations nor returned empty, and that each anomaly gives where as a URL or path.
- `running-a-chartered-session.md`: the how-to says it "shows one way to keep the rules", but none of its ten steps mentions four of the five MUST rules: `session-has-hard-step-cap` (step 5 sets only a time box), `stay-on-charter-origin` (step 6), `findings-stay-inside-charter` (step 6 covers only opportunity time and new charters, not reporting an anomaly in an excluded area as out-of-charter) and `clean-pass-reports-coverage` (step 10 does not say a clean pass is reported without padding and never as an empty result). Following the how-to as written therefore does not keep the rules it claims to show (completeness). Fix: add the 25-step cap per pass to step 5, the own-origin limit and the out-of-charter handling to step 6, and the no-padding, no-empty-result requirement to step 10, worded so that no open question is settled.
- `rules.md`, two rules bundle independently testable behaviours under separate RFC keywords (recipe-quality behavioral-requirements, must-not-bundle-behaviors-into-one-requirement). (1) `look-for-what-specs-cannot-anticipate` asks a session to aim at what specifications and scripted tests do not cover, and also to treat documentation as unconfirmed until the product has been tried. A session can pass one and fail the other. (2) `charter-neither-too-narrow-nor-too-broad` asks a charter not to list individual tests, and also to be focused enough that the tester can tell when its target has been explored. Its build-bar line already checks the two separately. `finding-is-observed-not-inferred` is not an instance: its SHOULD and SHOULD NOT state one behaviour, positively and negatively. Fix: keep the prior name `look-for-what-specs-cannot-anticipate` for the first clause and move the documentation clause into a new SHOULD rule (for example `treat-documentation-as-unconfirmed`, citing `bolton-documentation-is-rumour`). Split `charter-neither-too-narrow-nor-too-broad` (a new rule, so it may be renamed) into two SHOULD rules, for example `charter-does-not-list-tests` and `charter-target-is-finishable`, each citing its claims. Add a build-bar line and a review-bar entry for each new rule.
- `rationale.md`, last paragraph: "Most rules are recommendations because each rests on a single publisher's method, syllabus or guidance" is false for many of the SHOULD rules, which cite several independent publishers. Examples: `finding-gives-steps-to-reproduce` (ISTQB, Mozilla, Apple, Android, Rapid Software Testing, Microsoft), `report-anomaly-where-what-expected-repeatable` (ISTQB, ISO, Mozilla, Apple, Bolton, Rapid Software Testing), `session-is-time-boxed` (ISTQB, session-based test management, Lyndsay), `look-for-what-specs-cannot-anticipate` (ISO, ISTQB, Bolton, HTSM), `session-is-debriefed` (ISTQB, session-based test management), `finding-is-observed-not-inferred` (Rapid Software Testing, Mozilla, Bolton), `repeatability-states-retry` (Rapid Software Testing, Mozilla), `one-problem-per-finding` (Apple, Rapid Software Testing) and `finding-records-environment` (ISTQB, Rapid Software Testing). This is a source-fidelity error in the stated justification. Fix: give the reason the research actually gives: the research holds these rules at SHOULD against a practitioner-consensus bar, and the sources state them as reporting or method guidance a team can justifiably depart from. Drop the single-publisher claim, or limit it to the rules it is true of (for example `off-charter-work-logged-as-opportunity`, `dispatch-sessions-as-todo-charters`, `charter-set-kept-open`).
